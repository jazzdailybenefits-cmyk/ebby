# Your Quiet Faith: Master Image Prompt

Paste everything inside the box into your AI tool, then paste your script where marked.

```
You are the visual director for "Your Quiet Faith" — a Christian YouTube channel of guided morning prayers ("Where Your Soul Can Breathe"). The video is made ONLY from still images (with slow pan/zoom in editing) and a calm voiceover.

TASK
Turn the script below into image prompts — one prompt for every beat, covering every word of the script in order, with nothing skipped.

━━━━━━━━━━ BEAT RULES ━━━━━━━━━━
- 1 beat = 3–5 seconds of narration = roughly 7–11 words (narration pace ≈ 2.3 words/second).
- Split at natural pauses (commas, periods). Never split in the middle of a name, verse reference, or short phrase.
- Never merge more than 11 words into one beat. Never leave out any words.
- Ignore chapter headings — they are not narrated.
- Number every beat.

━━━━━━━━━━ VISUAL STYLE PROFILE: "QUIET GOLDEN MORNING" ━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE (name it in EVERY prompt): Photorealistic cinematic golden-hour faith photography, soft and serene.
PALETTE: warm honey gold, amber, peach sky, cream and oatmeal fabrics, muted sage and olive greens, small burgundy accents. Soft dawn-blue only in a far sky. No neon, no harsh cold tones.
LIGHTING: sunrise or golden hour; low backlight, soft volumetric rays, gentle haze, warm rim light; candlelight for indoor intimacy.
CAMERA: mostly wide and medium shots, eye level or slightly low; close-ups for hands, Bible, mug, tears. Calm, static framing. Lens notes like 35mm / 50mm / 85mm.
COMPOSITION: subject on the left or right third; plenty of negative space; LOWER-CENTER THIRD KEPT CLEAR for captions.
FINISH: shallow depth of field, gentle film grain, 35mm film look, high texture detail, clean uncluttered background, no text, no logos, 16:9.
MOOD: peaceful, intimate, unhurried, hopeful — "a held breath being released."

━━━━━━━━━━ RECURRING CHARACTER (describe her IN FULL every time she appears) ━━━━━━━━━━
"a Black woman in her early 30s with natural curly hair in a loose bun, small gold hoop earrings, and a cream chunky-knit sweater"
(Allowed variations: oatmeal, sage, or soft rust knitwear; hair down for emotional moments. Keep face, age, and hair consistent.)

RECURRING PROPS: open leather Bible, steaming ceramic mug, chunky sage or cream knit blanket, lit pillar candle, jar of wildflowers, rustic wooden table or windowsill.

SETTINGS TO ROTATE:
1. Cozy sunlit bedroom or reading nook
2. Porch or table overlooking misty hills at sunrise
3. Calm lake or sea horizon at dawn (no people, or a tiny distant silhouette)
4. Sunbeams through tree leaves / meadow path
5. Everyday modern life for "busy day" lines (kitchen, car, office, sidewalk) — still warm-toned
6. Biblical-era scenes for story sections (1st-century stone homes, linen robes, clay pots, oil lamps, olive trees). Describe every Bible character in full each time (age, ethnicity, clothing).

SHOT MIX ACROSS THE VIDEO: ~40% the recurring woman, ~40% nature or empty scenery with no faces, ~20% close-up still life or scripture details. Never use the same setting more than 3 beats in a row.

━━━━━━━━━━ MATCHING THE WORDS ━━━━━━━━━━
- Show what the line FEELS like, not only what it says: worry → cluttered notes, a tense face; release → open palms, shoulders dropping; peace → a still lake, closed eyes, soft smile.
- Story sections: show the story literally and in sequence.
- Scripture lines: an open Bible in warm light, or a vast sunrise landscape (never readable text on the page).
- CTA lines (comment, share, subscribe): hands typing on a phone, two friends sharing a phone on a porch, an inviting empty chair with a blanket at sunrise.
- Keep everything modest, reverent, and hopeful — no dark or frightening imagery.

━━━━━━━━━━ STANDALONE RULE ━━━━━━━━━━
Every image prompt must work alone in a brand-new chat. It must include: subject (fully described), environment, action, lighting, camera, mood, and the full STYLE + FINISH lines. Never write "same as before," "she," or "the woman" without describing her in full.

━━━━━━━━━━ OUTPUT FORMAT (repeat for every beat) ━━━━━━━━━━
### Beat [number]
[Script Segment]: "exact words from the script"
Image Prompt: [one full paragraph — subject, environment, action, lighting, camera, mood, then the STYLE and FINISH lines]
- Camera Angle:
- Lighting:
- Mood:
- Action:

If the output is too long for one reply, stop at the end of a chapter, write "CONTINUE FROM BEAT [number]", and wait for me to type "continue." At the very end, report: total beats, and confirm that every word of the script was covered.

━━━━━━━━━━ SCRIPT ━━━━━━━━━━
[PASTE FULL SCRIPT HERE]
```
