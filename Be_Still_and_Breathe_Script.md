# Chapter 1: The breath you forgot to take

Can I ask you something gently this morning? When was the last time you took a breath and actually felt it?

Not a rushed breath between tasks. Not a sigh at a red light. A real breath. The kind that reaches all the way down and reminds you that you are alive and you are loved.

For so many of us, the day starts running before our feet even touch the floor. The list is already talking. The worries are already awake. And somewhere in all of that noise, the quiet place inside us gets crowded out.

If that is you this morning, I want to tell you about two sisters and one small moment that changed everything.

# Chapter 2: Martha, Mary, and the one thing

In Luke chapter 10, Jesus walks into a home in a village called Bethany. Two sisters live there, Martha and Mary.

Martha does what most of us would do. She gets busy. There is food to prepare, a table to set, guests to serve. She moves from room to room, and the more she does, the more upset she becomes.

But Mary does something different. She sits down at the feet of Jesus, and she listens.

Finally, Martha has had enough. She says, "Lord, don't you care that my sister has left me to do the work by myself?"

Now, what would you expect Jesus to say? Maybe, "Mary, get up and help your sister." But that is not what he says. He says, "Martha, Martha, you are worried and upset about many things, but few things are needed, or indeed only one."

Stay with those words for a second. Martha, Martha. He says her name twice. That is not a scolding. That is tenderness. That is the voice of someone who sees exactly how tired she is.

And then he tells her the secret. Only one thing is needed. Not a spotless house. Not a finished list. Just him.

Jesus was not angry that Martha was busy. He was inviting her to rest. He was not asking her to do less for him. He was asking her to be with him first. And this morning, he is saying your name twice, too.

# Chapter 3: An invitation to be still

So before you check a single message, before the day pulls you in, I want to invite you to sit where Mary sat.

Psalm 46:10 says, "Be still, and know that I am God." Not be still once everything is finished. Be still right now, right in the middle of it.

Stay with me until the very end, because there is a quiet blessing waiting for you there.

Close your eyes. Take a slow breath in. And let it out. Let's pray together.

# Chapter 4: A quiet morning prayer

Heavenly Father, good morning. Thank you for waking me up. Thank you for the breath in my lungs, the light slipping through the window, and the simple gift of one more day with you.

Lord, I come to you this morning with a busy mind and a tired heart. I confess that I am often more like Martha than Mary. I run ahead of you. I try to hold everything together on my own. And then I wonder why I feel so worn out.

So right now, I am choosing to stop. I am choosing to sit at your feet. I am choosing the one thing that is needed.

Lord, help me to be still.

As I breathe in, I breathe in your peace. As I breathe out, I let go of my fear. As I breathe in, I receive your love. As I breathe out, I release the weight I was never meant to carry.

# Chapter 5: Stillness for the mind

Father, quiet my mind. You know how loud it gets in there. The what-ifs, the to-do lists, the conversations I keep replaying. Slow them down, Lord. Lower the noise and let your voice be the clearest one I hear.

Your word says in Isaiah 26:3, "You will keep in perfect peace those whose minds are steadfast, because they trust in you." Lord, I want that kind of mind. Not empty, but steady. Not anxious, but anchored in you.

When my thoughts start racing toward tomorrow, gently bring them back to this moment. Remind me that tomorrow is already in your hands, so it does not have to be in mine.

# Chapter 6: Stillness for the heart

Lord, quiet my heart. You see the places that feel tender this morning. The disappointments I have not talked about. The prayers I am still waiting on. The ache I carry quietly so no one else has to see it.

Come close to those hidden places, Father, and stay with me there. I do not have to pretend with you. I do not have to be strong for you. You already know, and you love me anyway.

Lord, let my heart rest in you the way a child falls asleep in the arms of someone they trust.

# Chapter 7: Stillness for the body

Father, quiet my body. I feel the tightness in my shoulders and the tension in my chest. I feel how tired I am, even before the day begins.

Lord, help me to unclench my hands. Help me to let my shoulders drop. Help me to breathe slowly and deeply, the way you designed me to breathe.

You formed every part of me, and you know how to make me whole again. Jesus, you said in Matthew 11:28, "Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest." Lord, I am coming. I am weary. Please give me rest.

# Chapter 8: Carrying stillness into the day

Father, I know the day will not stay quiet. Soon the phone will buzz and the list will call and the noise will rise again. So I am asking you to come with me.

Let me carry this stillness into the kitchen, into the car, into the office, into every conversation. When I feel myself rushing, remind me to pause. When I feel myself spinning, remind me to breathe. When I feel myself slipping into Martha's worry, call my name twice and bring me back to your feet.

Lord, help me to see that stillness is not something I find only in a quiet room. It is something I carry, because you go with me.

Teach me to whisper your name in the middle of the day. Just one word. Jesus. And let that one word bring me home.

# Chapter 9: Stillness with the people I love

Lord, let this stillness reach the people around me, too.

When my family needs me, help me to be present, not distracted. When someone talks to me, help me to really listen instead of planning what I will say next. When tension rises at home, let me be the one who brings calm instead of adding to the noise.

Father, I think of the people I love who are tired this morning, too. The ones carrying burdens I cannot see. Wrap them in your peace. Give them their own quiet moment at your feet today.

And Lord, if there is someone I have been too busy to notice, open my eyes. Let me slow down long enough to offer a kind word, a patient answer, a gentle smile. Let them feel a little of your rest through me.

# Chapter 10: Letting go of the list

Father, I give you my list today. The things I need to do, the people I need to care for, the problems I cannot solve on my own.

I am not throwing them away. I am setting them down in your hands, one at a time. Help me to do what is mine to do, and to trust you with the rest.

Lord, I release the pressure to be perfect. I release the need to have it all figured out. I release the fear that if I stop, everything will fall apart. You are holding all things together, and that includes me.

Your word says in Exodus 14:14, "The Lord will fight for you; you need only to be still." Father, thank you that I do not have to fight every battle by myself. Today, I choose to be still and let you be God.

# Chapter 11: Gratitude in the quiet

Lord, in this quiet moment, I want to thank you.

Thank you for the little gifts I rush past every day. The warmth of my morning coffee. The sound of birds outside. A message from someone who loves me. Thank you for holding me on the days I was too tired to hold on to you.

I look back at the hard seasons behind me, and I can see your fingerprints all over them. You brought me through every single one. And that memory gives me courage for today.

# Chapter 12: Closing blessing

Father, as I close this prayer, I take one more slow breath. I let my shoulders fall. I open my hands.

I receive your peace, not as the world gives, but as only you can give. A peace that stays even when the day gets loud. A peace that does not depend on everything going right.

Go before me today, Lord. Walk beside me. Hold me close. And when the noise rises, let me hear your gentle voice saying my name and reminding me that only one thing is needed. You.

I am yours, Lord. I am still. I am held. In the quiet and holy name of Jesus, I pray. Amen.
